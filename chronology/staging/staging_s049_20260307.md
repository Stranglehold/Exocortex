## 5. Jake's Reframe: Navigation, Not Classification

**What happened:** At the end of the session, after seeing the centroid distances and the Rorschach blots, Jake said: "The multi-mode categorization isn't a flaw — it's telling you start and end points, and that there is a path to each of them. The journey beats the destination."

**What it means:** This reframes the entire BST and prosthetic cortex from a classification system to a navigation system. The instrument doesn't label inputs. It shows where they are in a continuous space and which directions are available for exploration. Multi-domain signals aren't ambiguity to resolve. They're paths to explore.

**What it means for SOUL.md:** The project's thesis evolves from "deterministic scaffolding compensates for model limitations" to something more expansive: "the representational geometry is navigable territory, and the prosthetic makes navigation possible where the model can't navigate alone." The scaffolding metaphor is being superseded by the navigation metaphor. Both are true. The navigation metaphor is more complete.

**Status:** Single articulation. Profound but needs to be tested against the build — does the navigation framing produce better interventions than the classification framing? The data from the instrument will answer this.
