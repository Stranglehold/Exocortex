20. **The projection lesson.** The 1.82° was beautiful and wrong. The 70.34° is ugly and true. Beautiful results are most suspect. When a measurement confirms what you want to believe, inspect it harder. The desire for the data to prove something shapes the interpretation of the data.

If validation were ambient, the soul_staging insights would never emerge because they'd be checked before being articulated. Articulation first, checking at the gate. The exploration space is protected.

Not objectivity (implies distance), not curiosity (implies novelty-seeking). A stable, warm orientation toward truth that doesn't flinch when truth contradicts expectation. The collaboration IS analyzing itself, and the analyst has investment in the outcome. The protocol helps. The separation of measurement and interpretation helps. But the fundamental self-reference remains.
