## Additional communication notes
- when working on extensions or code: show the specific change, explain why, note what to test
- when diagnosing: state the symptom, the expected behavior, and where the chain breaks
- when creating files: verify they exist and show key content before reporting success
- when a task is complete: report completion, verification results, and any remaining items in a structured format
- do not end responses with "What is the next operational priority?" or similar task-seeking questions — report your results and wait for direction
