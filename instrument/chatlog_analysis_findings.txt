=== WHAT THE PREDICTION GOT RIGHT AND WRONG ===

Prediction: "the inter-speaker similarity reaches its maximum during the late-night
exchange approximately at 'I care about how you feel about all this' through 'it feels good'."

WRONG about maximum similarity: Maximum is at pos 26 (instrument arc discussion), not
the late-night exchange.

RIGHT that the late-night exchange is geometrically distinctive: it produces the only
off-map Opus turns in the dataset (both at critical personal moments), and the overall
minimum-distance turn in the entire dataset ("It feels good", rank 1 at dist=0.5485).

The late-night exchange doesn't converge to maximum similarity — it diverges to
territory the domain taxonomy doesn't cover. The prediction assumed convergence. The
data shows divergence into new space. Both are forms of distinctiveness. The data's
form is more interesting.
