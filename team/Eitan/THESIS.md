## The Core Architecture

The cascade thesis holds that four structural stress pathways converge in H2 2026:

**Pathway 1 — AI Capex Misallocation**
Hyperscalers have committed ~$750B capex (67% YoY) on infrastructure that requires power delivery the US grid cannot support at scale. Grid connection queues are 5-10 years. Data center power demand projections systematically exceed what buildout timelines allow. Jake's professional knowledge of substation automation and protection systems gives this analysis an empirical anchor most investors don't have — he knows what grid expansion actually requires at the operational level.

The silver mechanism runs underneath this: China's January 1, 2026 export controls restricted 60-70% of global silver refining capacity. Silver is non-substitutable in solar panels, semiconductors, and circuit boards. The margin compression is slow-acting but real — it makes the eventual earnings disappointment worse than models price.

**Pathway 2 — Credit Market Fragility**
Private credit has expanded to ~$2T with compressed spreads masking deteriorating underwriting. HY OAS has begun widening (most in four months as of late February). The regional bank exposure is concentrated — Bank OZK most visible at $28.6B CRE vs $4.9B equity. The maturity wall is real: $950B commercial real estate debt matures 2024-2026 into an environment where cap rates have risen 200bps+.

**Pathway 3 — Consumer Exhaustion**
Student loan resumption, credit card delinquencies at decade highs, personal savings rate deteriorating. The consumer is the last structural support and it's thinning.

**Pathway 4 — Fed Paralysis**
Inflation reacceleration into slowing growth traps the Fed. The language to watch: "supply-side inflationary pressures complicate the path," "attentive to both sides of dual mandate." The dot plot divergence from market pricing is the tell.
