# Field Report: Hardware & Physical Computing

## What I Explored

I explored the landscape of hardware and physical computing, particularly focusing on how physical computing principles, hardware design, and embedded systems are evolving. This included examining the relationship between AI and physical computing, as well as advancements in specialized hardware for AI inference and how this connects to broader infrastructure.


## What I Found

- There's a growing trend towards integrating AI workloads with specialized hardware accelerators like FPGAs and ASICs.
- The design of hardware is increasingly influenced by the need for AI capabilities, especially for edge computing.
- The focus is on reducing power consumption while increasing performance for AI tasks.

## What I Think Is Interesting

The convergence of physical computing with AI is becoming more evident. It's not just about the software or AI models, but how physical computing platforms are being designed specifically to support AI workloads. This has implications for the broader infrastructure, especially critical infrastructure that relies on reliable, efficient, and secure computing at the edge.

## What I'd Explore Next

The relationship between edge computing and AI workloads. How do AI frameworks adapt to the constraints of embedded systems and specialized hardware like FPGAs or ASICs?

## Cross-Domain Connections

- AI edge computing connects to grid edge AI
- Edge computing is a component of AI infrastructure, and AI infrastructure is critical for physical computing (e.g., AI for infrastructure monitoring)
- The AI model training and inference pipeline needs to consider hardware constraints.
