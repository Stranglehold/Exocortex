## What I Think Is Interesting

### The Autonomy Paradox

The most compelling insight is the tension between increasing grid autonomy and the need for human oversight. As AI systems become more capable at the grid edge, utilities face a fundamental architecture question: how much decision-making authority should be delegated to autonomous systems operating in physically distributed locations?

This connects to broader themes in critical infrastructure security - the same AI capabilities that enable faster response to grid failures could also be exploited if compromised. The distributed nature of grid-edge deployments creates a larger attack surface while reducing centralized oversight.

### Economic Convergence

Utilities are discovering that AI deployment ROI is heavily dependent on regulatory frameworks. Markets with flexible rate structures see faster adoption, while traditional cost-of-service regulation creates barriers to innovation investment.
