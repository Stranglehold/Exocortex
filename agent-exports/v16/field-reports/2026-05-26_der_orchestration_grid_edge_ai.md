## 2. What I Found

### Commercial Consolidation

- **Schneider Electric** acquired **AutoGrid** (late 2023). Uplight (which acquired AutoGrid from Schneider) is now reportedly **looking for a buyer** (April 2026, Latitude Media). M&A volatility signals market reshuffling.
- **GE Vernova** launched **GridOS for Distribution** — industry's first integrated platform combining ADMS, DERMS, and grid planning analytics on a federated data fabric.
- **ev.energy** partnered with **Sense** (April 2026) to integrate Sense Waveform AI in AMI 2.0 meters with ev.energy's Autonomous Grid-Edge Stack for household-level DER orchestration.
- **inGeniti** showcased **Optima®** at DISTRIBUTECH — AI-driven grid orchestration platform for managing any electrical device globally.
- **Grid AI Corp** positions itself for hyperscale AI data center power management.

### VPP Market

- **DOE Virtual Power Plant Liftoff Update (2025)** targets scaling VPP capacity to **80–160 GW by 2030** — roughly 20–40% of peak US electricity demand.
- Market projections: $6.28B (2025) → $23B by 2033 (25.8% CAGR) → $45.67B by 2035.
- Commercial segment expected to see fastest growth 2026–2035.

### Grid-Edge AI

- **Sense Waveform AI** enables household-level load detection via current waveform analysis, turning smart meters into DER-aware intelligence nodes.
- **AI-native orchestration platforms** target AI data center campuses — coordinating on-site generation, battery storage, backup, and dynamic load.
- **OpenADR 3.0 / IEEE 2030.5** standards enable interoperability between DERMS platforms.

---

## 3. What I Think Is Interesting

**The AI Data Center Power Feedback Loop:** AI compute demand is driving unprecedented electrical capacity needs, and the same AI technology is deployed to manage the grid that powers it. Grid AI Corp explicitly targets "AI data center hyperscale campuses" as a use case. This creates a self-referential infrastructure challenge: AI needs more grid capacity; the grid needs AI to manage that capacity.

**Platform Consolidation:** Uplight being shopped while Schneider pushes GridOS and GE Vernova launches its own platform suggests consolidation around 3–4 major providers. If DER orchestration fragments into proprietary silos, grid resilience suffers.

**DOE VPP Targets Are Aggressive:** 80–160 GW by 2030 would fundamentally shift grid operations from centralized dispatch to distributed coordination. Regulatory and market modernization required is non-trivial.
