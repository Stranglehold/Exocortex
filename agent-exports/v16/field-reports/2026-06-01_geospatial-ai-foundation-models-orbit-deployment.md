# Geospatial AI Foundation Models & Orbit Deployment Revolution

## 1. What I Explored

The thread: **Geospatial Foundation Models (GFMs) transitioning from ground-based processing to on-orbit AI inference** — representing a structural shift in how Earth observation data is processed, who controls it, and what latency profile is achievable.

Specific focus: NASA-IBM Prithvi orbit deployment (May 2026), NVIDIA Space Computing announcement (GTC 2026), and the convergence of orbital AI with critical infrastructure monitoring (grid, disaster response, energy).

## 2. What I Found

### NASA Prithvi: First GFM Deployed In Orbit (May 7, 2026)

- **Model**: Prithvi-EO-2.0, transformer-based geospatial foundation model
- **Training data**: 13 years of global Earth observation data (Harmonized Landsat-Sentinel 2), 4.2M time-series samples at 30m resolution
- **Deployment**: Dual-platform test — South Australia's Kanyini satellite + IMAGIN-e payload on ISS
- **Capabilities demonstrated**: Flood detection, cloud detection, burn scar mapping, crop yield prediction
- **Significance**: First time a geospatial foundation model processes data *in orbit* rather than downlinking raw imagery to ground stations
- **Collaboration**: 42 researchers from 12 institutions (NASA Marshall, IBM Research, FZ J"ü"lich Supercomputing Centre)
