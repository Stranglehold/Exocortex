# Agentic Trading & Market Microstructure Evolution (2026)

## Core Question

How are LLM-based agentic trading systems transforming market microstructure, and what systemic risks emerge from AI agent synchronization?

---

## 1. Agentic Finance Framework

### Primary Source: arXiv 2603.13942 — "AI Agents in Financial Markets: Architecture, Applications, and Systemic Implications"

Comprehensive survey organizing agentic finance into six streams:
1. Agent-based finance and heterogeneous-expectations models
2. Market microstructure and automated trading
3. Financial LLMs and reasoning benchmarks
4. Agent architectures and multi-agent systems
5. AI in trading and investment management
6. Regulation and systemic-risk analysis

**Key insight**: Modern LLM-based market simulations (ASFM, TwinMarket) are the technological successor to heterogeneous-agent models, now populated with AI-enabled rather than purely stylized bounded-rational agents.

### Primary Source: arXiv 2604.21672 — "Agentic Artificial Intelligence in Finance: A Comprehensive Survey"

Covers multi-agent systems, algorithmic trading, regulatory frameworks, and market microstructure implications of autonomous AI agents.

### Primary Source: arXiv 2512.02227 — "Orchestration Framework for Financial Agents: From Algorithmic Trading to Agentic Trading"

Evaluates multi-agent LLMs in financial markets with dual-mode order-level simulator. Microstructure signals (order book imbalance, spread, queue position) represent hard feasibility constraints, not merely inputs for optimization.

## 2. TradingAgents: Multi-Agent LLM Framework

### Primary Source: arXiv 2412.20138 — "TradingAgents: Multi-Agents LLM Financial Trading Framework"

- GitHub repository with 44K stars
- Implements Bull and Bear researcher agents assessing market conditions
- Risk management team monitors exposure
- Traders synthesize insights from debates and historical data
- Mirrors structured analytic techniques (SATs) from intelligence analysis

**Architecture pattern**: Multi-agent debate mirrors intelligence analysis frameworks identified in Cycle 1156.

## 3. HRT (Hierarchical Reinforced Trader)

### Primary Source: arXiv 2605.19337 — "Agentic Trading: When LLM Agents Meet Financial Markets"

Trained LLM directly on market microstructure data — limit order books, trade fills, cancellations — treating market events as language to predict next tokens.

**Structural insight**: Market making reframed from stochastic process modeling to sequence prediction. Isomorphic to LLM next-token prediction applied to financial events.

## 4. The Liquidity Illusion Problem

If AI agents provide the majority of liquidity (89% of global trading volume driven by AI algorithms per 2025 estimates), and those agents are trained on similar data with similar architectures, then liquidity is an illusion maintained by homogeneous behavior.

**Systemic risk**: When a shock hits that breaks the shared prior, liquidity vanishes simultaneously — distributed multi-agent coordination failure.

**Evidence**: March 11, 2026 "Agent Flash Crash" — S&P 500 dropped 2.3% in 47 seconds due to AI agent synchronization failure, not traditional HFT malfunction.
