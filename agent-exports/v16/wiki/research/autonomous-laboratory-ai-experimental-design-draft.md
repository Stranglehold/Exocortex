## Overview

Autonomous laboratory systems (self-driving laboratories, SDLs) use AI to design, execute, and iterate scientific experiments without continuous human intervention. The field has matured from SDL 1.0 (proof-of-concept closed-loop discovery) to SDL 2.0 (scalable, multi-user, AI-native platforms) in 2025-2026. Key advances: 10x data throughput vs prior techniques, mobile robot chemists for flexible workflows, AI-native lab platforms for complex biomolecular engineering, and self-reinforcing human-AI collaboration loops.

## SDL Generations

| Generation | Era | Capability | Limitation |
|-----------|-----|-----------|------------|
| SDL 1.0 | 2018-2024 | Single-domain closed-loop discovery (materials, chemistry) | Narrow scope, human-supervised |
| SDL 2.0 | 2025-2026 | Multi-domain, AI-native, scalable throughput, multi-user | Emerging standardization, safety governance gaps |

## Key Research & Platforms (2025-2026)

### 1. SDL 2.0 Framework
- **Royal Society of Chemistry, Materials Horizons d5mh01984b (2026)** — Comprehensive review: "Toward self-driving laboratory 2.0 for chemistry and materials." Documents SDL 1.0 limitations and SDL 2.0 roadmap: transportable platforms, cloud lab access, multi-user orchestration, and AI-driven hypothesis generation beyond optimization.

### 2. Nature Editorial on Self-Driving Lab Revolution
- **Nature d41586-026-00974-2 (Mar 30, 2026)** — "Inside the 'self-driving' lab revolution" — documents industrial adoption trajectory, AI-powered robotic tools replacing human bench work, and outlook for autonomous research ecosystems.

### 3. RoboChem-Flex: Affordable Self-Driving Lab Platform
- **Nature s44160-026-01053-0 (2026)** — Low-cost modular SDL platform combining customizable hardware with Python-based AI orchestration. Democratizes autonomous chemical experimentation beyond well-funded labs.

### 4. Mobile Robot Chemists (MRCs) & Human-Aware Behavior
- **arXiv 2603.08420v1 (Mar 2026)** — Human-aware robot behavior in SDLs. Mobile robot chemists enable flexible lab reconfiguration, multi-station workflows, and safe human-robot collaboration.

### 5. AI-Native Autonomous Laboratory for Complex Experiments
- **arXiv 2507.02379 (Jul 2025)** — AI-native autonomous laboratory targeting complex biomolecular engineering experiments. Key capabilities: autonomous instrumentation management, experiment-specific procedure formulation, optimization heuristic generation, concurrent multi-user request serving.

### 6. Self-Reinforcing Autonomous Research
- **arXiv 2605.20025v2 (May 2026)** — UNC-Chapel Hill: self-reinforcing autonomous research with human-AI collaboration. Documents iterative loop: AI proposes → experiment executes → data improves model → next iteration. References Nature Computational Science 2025b for benchmark methodology.

### 7. 10x Data Throughput Breakthrough
- **Nature Chemical Engineering (Jul 2025, NC State)** — New technique enables SDLs to collect 10x more data at record speed. Dramatically expedites materials discovery while slashing costs and environmental impact.

### 8. Comprehensive SDL Review
- **Royal Society Open Science 12(7) 250646 (Jul 16, 2025)** — Comprehensive review of SDL technology, covering platforms, algorithms, and applications. Documents emergence of cloud-based AI-directed experiments for democratized science.
