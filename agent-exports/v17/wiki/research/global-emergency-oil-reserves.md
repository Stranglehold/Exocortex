## Overview

The US Strategic Petroleum Reserve is the world's largest single emergency stockpile, but it operates inside a wider multilateral defense-in-depth system for oil security: the International Energy Agency (IEA) coordinated emergency reserve network created after the 1973-74 OPEC embargo. The 2026 Hormuz crisis produced the largest coordinated release in the system's history (US 172M bbl SPR drawdown plus IEA 400M bbl made available) and stress-tested the architecture to the point of buffer exhaustion. This page documents the global mechanism, the 2026 record activation, the non-IEA national stockpile layer (China, Japan, India, Korea, EU), and the buffer-exhaustion lesson for macro monitoring. It is the international complement to the US-centric [[strategic-petroleum-reserve]] page.

## 1. IEA Mechanism & Legal Architecture

- **Origin:** IEA founded 1974 in Paris under the International Energy Program (IEP) Treaty — the institutional answer to the 1973-74 embargo that created the US SPR.
- **Core obligation:** members maintain emergency oil stocks equivalent to 90 days of net imports (crude + refined products); in many members industry stocks count toward the requirement.
- **System scale:** IEA members hold roughly 4B bbl of total government + industry stocks; ~1.2-1.5B bbl are government/agency-controlled. The US SPR (714M bbl capacity) is the largest single component.
- **Emergency Response System toolbox:** demand restraint, fuel switching, surge production, stockdraw (the primary lever), and allocation.
- **Activation precedents:** 2005 Hurricane Katrina (first coordinated release), 2011 Libya (60M bbl), 2022 Russia invasion (coordinated 120M bbl: US 60M + ~60M from partners at unprecedented pace). 2022 set the template the 2026 Hormuz activation followed.
- **Trigger doctrine:** the IEP 7% supply-loss threshold is a treaty criterion, but in practice activation decisions are political judgments by the Governing Board — a structural friction that matters for forecasting (announcement-to-barrels conversion).

## 2. 2026 Hormuz Crisis: Record Coordinated Release

Corpus-grounded (v16/v17 Exocortex pages; IEA OMR Mar 12 2026; Brookings May 22 2026; EIA WPSR Aug 5 2026):

- **March 11 2026:** US authorized 172M bbl SPR release — largest single-country emergency release since 1973 (DOE).
- **Same day:** IEA members agreed to make 400M bbl of emergency reserves available; of the global commitment, 301M bbl was crude oil (Brookings).
- **Release pace:** ~2.5 mb/d buffer over a four-month window.
- **Coverage math:** against the estimated 8.5M bpd global inventory drawdown, the 400M bbl commitment covered ~16 days ([[oil-market-disruption-2026-macro-scenarios]]).
- **Mechanics** documented in the IEA Oil Market Report (Mar 12 2026).
- **Loan compounding:** June 2026 reporting — "SPR Borrowers Owe Uncle Sam 40 Million Extra Barrels"; exchange-loan repayments extend the de facto drawdown beyond headline releases.
- **Buffer exhaustion:** mid-July 2026 the IEA crude release buffer was nearly exhausted; US SPR hit 304.8M bbl on July 31 2026, lowest since 1983 (EIA WPSR Aug 5 2026).

## 3. Non-IEA & National Reserve Nodes

- **China:** largest opaque holder. Structural baseline: roughly 475-510M bbl total (government + commercial) on 2017-2020 estimates, with phased cavern/above-ground buildout toward 850M+ bbl capacity. China does not publish SPR levels; buying/refill is inferred from import flows and is price-sensitive — a de facto price floor at dips. Current 2026 levels unverified (honest gap).
- **Japan:** JOGMEC-led national stockholding; among the deepest per-consumption buffers (~100+ days of imports, ~300M+ bbl).
- **India:** small strategic layer — 5 SPR caverns (~36.5M bbl, ~9-10 days cover) plus commercial stockholding; Phase 2 (~53M bbl) under construction; long-run target near 90 days including commercial.
- **South Korea:** ~90-100 days of cover via national + industry stocks.
- **EU/Europe:** member states hold Type A/B/C stocks (agency/commercial/crisis) under EU/UE-OSHD rules synchronized with the IEA 90-day obligation.

## 4. Refill Dynamics & Price Effects

- **US refill:** slow and price-capped — DOE ~1M bbl purchase contracts (Nov 12 2025; deliveries Dec 2025-Jan 2026, Bryan Mound) at ≤$79/bbl target; inventories: Dec 2025 413M → Apr 2026 ~397.9M → Jul 31 2026 304.8M.
- **Strategic refill as price floor:** coordinated stockdraw caps spot spikes; announced refill programs floor dips — the crude analogue of a central-bank put. This interacts with futures term structure ([[commodity-futures-term-structure]]) and with weekly EIA WPSR data as a nowcasting surface ([[alternative-data-sources-financial-intelligence]]).
- **Buffer theory:** emergency stocks are a stochastic inventory buffer. The 2026 exhaustion is the energy-sector analogue of reserve-scarcity events in money markets — the Cleveland Fed inventory-calibration finding (policymakers act as if too-few reserves cost ~20× too-many) transfers directly ([[federal-reserve-repo-market-mechanics]]).
