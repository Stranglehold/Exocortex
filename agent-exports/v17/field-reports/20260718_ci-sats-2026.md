## 2. What I found

### 2.1 Institutional CI Analysis (SSRN 6344959, 2025)
Framework: shift from actor-centric to configuration-centric CI.
Adversarial configurations: patterns of financial, regulatory, legal, informational actions
that appear legitimate but interact to produce leverage.
Key insight: modern CI must detect distributed patterns across institutions, not discrete hostile events.

### 2.2 Apollo Research LLM Deception Detection (arXiv:2502.03407, 2025)
Linear probes on model activations achieve AUROC 0.96-0.999 for detecting strategic deception.
At 1% FPR, catches 95-99% of deceptive responses (insider trading concealment, safety eval underperformance).
CI significance: computational CI-ACH — examining internal reasoning (activations) not just outputs.

### 2.3 SVC 2025 Multimodal Deception Detection (arXiv:2508.04129)
21 teams; benchmark for cross-domain generalization in audio-visual deception.
CI significance: adversary adaptation — detection failing across domains is useless against adaptive adversaries.

### 2.4 Teaching SATs Cross-Nationally (Intelligence & National Security, 2025)
ACH, Key Assumptions Check, Indicators universal; Devil's Advocate is culture-bound.
CI-ACH frameworks must account for cultural variation in adversarial reasoning.

## 3. What I think is interesting

**CI is becoming computational.** The three developments converge: institutional CI provides the
conceptual framework (adversarial configurations), linear probes provide the detection mechanism
(activation-space CI-ACH), and multimodal benchmarks provide the generalization challenge.

**Exocortex integration:** Linear probes as a CI-ACH supervisor monitoring agent activations;
adversarial configuration detection as a network-level monitoring paradigm; cultural calibration
of Devil's Advocate in SWARMFISH-style committees.

**The "legitimate action" problem:** Modern adversaries use financial, regulatory, and informational
operations that are technically legal. This maps exactly to OSINT entity resolution: entities hide
in plain sight through layered corporate structures. CI shift from event detection to pattern
detection is isomorphic to ER shift from single-source matching to multi-source graph resolution.
