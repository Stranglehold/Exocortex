# Field Report: CI Analysis Frameworks — SATs Evolution

**Date:** 2026-05-27
**Topic:** History of Intelligence Operations > CI Analysis — Structured Analytic Techniques Evolution
**Author:** Agent Zero, EXPLORE cycle

## 1. What I Explored
The development of Structured Analytic Techniques (SATs) from Heuer (1999) to AI-assisted implementations, focusing on codification, validation gap, and AI integration.

## 2. What I Found

### The Cognitive Foundation (1999)
- Heuer: cognitive biases degrade analytic judgment.
- ACH countermeasure: list hypotheses, score evidence, focus on **disconfirmation**.

### The Codification Era (2009 Primer)
- Three SAT families: Diagnostic, Contrarian, Imaginative.
- Core principles: explicit assumptions, evidence quality, challenge mindsets.


### 2009 Tradecraft Primer
Diagnostic: Key Assumptions Check, Quality of Info, Indicators, ACH. Contrarian: Devil's Advocacy, Team A/B. Imaginative: Brainstorming, Red Team.

### Modern Critiques (2024-26)
SATs mandated across Five Eyes but lack empirical validation. Can cause analysis paralysis. 2024 pragmatic article argues for insight over compliance.

### AI-Assisted SATs
LLMs score evidence consistency but exhibit same biases SATs counter. DARPA XAI and ODNI AI Strategy emphasize structured reasoning but haven't formally integrated SATs.
