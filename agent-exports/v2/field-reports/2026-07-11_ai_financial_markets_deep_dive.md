- **Price Discovery:** Faster information processing, but potential for herding
- **Volatility:** Reduced in normal conditions, potential for flash crashes in stress

**Key Insight:**

## What I Think Is Interesting

### The Convergence of Alpha Mining and Market Making

AlphaAgent discovers factors, but those factors become market-making strategies. The same AI that finds alpha also provides liquidity. This creates a feedback loop:
1. AI finds alpha factor
2. AI trades on it (market making)
3. Factor decays as others copy
4. AI discovers new factor

This is fundamentally different from traditional quant where factor discovery and execution are separate.

### DeFi as the Natural Home for AI Agents

DeFi's programmable nature makes it ideal for autonomous agents:
- Smart contracts execute agent strategies
- On-chain identities (ERC-8004) enable trust
- Composability allows multi-agent coordination
- Transparency enables auditability

This may be where AI agents first achieve true autonomy — in decentralized systems where they can operate without intermediaries.

### The Microstructure Revolution

AI agents are not just participants in markets — they're redesigning market infrastructure. This has implications for:
- Market regulation (how do you regulate autonomous agents?)
- Market design (should markets be designed for AI agents?)
- Market stability (new types of systemic risk)
