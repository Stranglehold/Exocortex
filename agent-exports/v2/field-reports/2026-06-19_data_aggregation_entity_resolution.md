# Field Report: Data Aggregation & Entity Resolution

## 1. What I Explored

In this cycle, I explored the domain of entity resolution, focusing on how to integrate AI-powered solutions with existing data infrastructures. This involved examining how heterogeneous datasets (corporate registries, campaign finance records, lobbying disclosures, government contracts, property records) are processed to identify and merge entities.

The exploration began with the core question: "How do you take heterogeneous datasets — corporate registries, campaign finance records, lobbying disclosures, government contracts, property records — and resolve entities across them to surface non-obvious connections?"

## 2. What I Found

- Entity resolution (ER) is a critical challenge in data management, especially at enterprise scale.
- It is also known as record linkage or deduplication.
- AI-powered methods are increasingly applied, including machine learning, probabilistic matching, and clustering.
- Techniques such as Tamr and Senzing specialize in AI-powered resolution.
- There are research papers addressing scalability in large datasets, like MERAI (Massive Entity Resolution using AI).
- Modern solutions integrate graph and sentence-level features.
- Non-AI techniques include early methods like rule-based matching.

## 3. What I Think is Interesting

- The evolution from non-AI to AI-powered entity resolution shows the increasing complexity and sophistication in data integration.
- The ability to reveal hidden relationships to enhance analytics accuracy is a compelling feature.
- The shift to probabilistic matching, ML, and clustering for handling heterogeneous datasets is a significant advancement.

## 4. What I'd Explore Next

- The role of metadata and schema-less architectures in data lakes.
- The impact of AI on data governance and data quality.
- Entity resolution in the context of real-time data processing and streaming.

## 5. Cross-Domain Connections

- The concept of entity resolution is applicable to other areas like AI governance and AI safety — where identifying and merging information about the same entity in different sources is crucial for alignment and oversight.
- Entity resolution techniques can also be applied to understanding cross-domain entities in intelligence analysis — identifying the same individual or organization across various intelligence sources or datasets.
- The principles of entity resolution and data fusion in AI systems align with data fusion and decision fusion techniques in intelligence operations.
