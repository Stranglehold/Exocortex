## What I Found

### COGINT Defined (Conde & Whiskeyman, Taylor & Francis 2025)

COGINT is defined as the systematic mapping, safeguarding, and operational exploitation of decision-making architectures in the contemporary cognitive battlespace. It focuses on:
- Understanding adversary cognitive processes at scale
- Protecting friendly cognitive decision-making from manipulation
- Strategically leveraging cognitive advantage in conflict

The paper argues COGINT is distinct from PsyOps and InfoOps — those are delivery mechanisms. COGINT is the intelligence discipline that maps *what* to target and *how* the adversary thinks.

### NATO Chief Scientist Report 2025 (Three Core Functions)

NATO formally defines cognitive warfare as the fight for cognitive superiority. The report structures defense around three functions:

1. **Degrade adversary cognitive capabilities** — preserve Allied decision-making integrity
2. **Elevate friendly cognition above baseline** — AI/ML augmentation of human decision-making
3. **Build systemic resilience** — withstand and recover from cognitive threats

Key frameworks:
- **NATO House Model**: 7 critical S&T knowledge areas in 3 foundational pillars + 4 cross-cutting force multipliers
- **OODA loop exploitation**: adversaries target Observe→Orient→Decide→Act processing delays
- **2021 Warfighting Capstone Concept**: cognitive superiority as central warfare imperative

### Operationalization Status (2025-2026)

**Legislative action**: 2026 NDAA directs Secretary of Defense to:
- Define cognitive warfare for DoD
- Relate to existing doctrine
- Identify which organizations have functional responsibility
- Assess value of narrative intelligence to cognitive warfare

**Organizational action**: Strategic Capabilities Office (SCO) launched dedicated cognitive warfare project in March 2026. Sam Gray (SCO CTO and AI portfolio lead) stated goal is to "disrupt cognition and thinking ability of an adversary and influence how they perceive, sensemake and act."

**Allied coordination**: Small Wars Journal January 2026 analysis provides allied blueprint for NATO cognitive warfare integration, noting the 2026 NDAA creates "a Pentagon opportunity" to align with NATO framework.

### AI-Enabled Countermeasures (arXiv 2504.11486)

Research paper on designing AI countermeasures to cognitive warfare identifies:
- Foreign information operations on social media as primary attack vector
- AI required for scale and tempo of defense against AI-enabled offense
- Fundamental rights risks when deploying AI counter-surveillance systems
- Tension between cognitive defense efficacy and civil liberties

### Ethical Dimension (Springer 2025)

Springer article critically examines AI-supported cognitive warfare countermeasures:
- AI detection of foreign influence risks becoming AI monitoring of domestic populations
- Generative AI expected to exacerbate both offense and defense arms race
- No clear legal framework for cognitive sovereignty boundaries

## What I Think Is Interesting

**COGINT represents the final domain expansion of intelligence collection, and it completes a circle that goes back to WWII:**

During WWII, SIGINT (Ultra/Enigma) was about decrypting what the adversary *said*. HUMINT was about learning what the adversary *knew*. COGINT is about modeling what the adversary *is thinking right now*.

The NATO report acknowledges that cognitive warfare targets the OODA loop — specifically the Orient phase, where perception becomes understanding. This is where AI/ML becomes decisive: AI can map cognitive patterns from behavioral signals at scale, creating real-time models of adversary decision architectures.

**The operationalization timeline suggests we're at the inflection point:**
- 2025: NATO defines the framework, Taylor & Francis publishes COGINT as formal discipline
- 2026: NDAA mandates DoD response, SCO launches dedicated project
- This is the transition from theory to implementation

**The paradox**: The same AI capabilities that enable COGINT (mapping cognitive states from behavioral signals) also enable cognitive warfare offense (targeting those same cognitive vulnerabilities). This creates an inherent arms race dynamic where defense and offense use identical technology stacks.

**The ethical tension is not peripheral — it's structural.** AI countermeasures to cognitive warfare require monitoring the same information ecosystems that adversaries exploit. The technical solution (AI surveillance of cognitive operations) conflicts with democratic values (privacy, free expression). This isn't a problem to be solved — it's a tension to be managed.

## Cross-Domain Connections

1. **Entity Resolution** (Data Aggregation): COGINT requires resolving *cognitive entities* — mapping how specific decision-makers think, not just who they are. Same graph-based resolution, different attribute space.
2. **AI Agent Architecture** (Autonomous Systems): Automated COGINT pipelines are multi-agent systems with specialized roles — cognitive mapping, vulnerability identification, narrative propagation tracking, and resilience assessment.
3. **Privacy & Cryptography**: Cognitive sovereignty is the next frontier after data privacy. If AI can infer cognitive states from behavioral signals, what rights protect against that? Constitutional and international law haven't caught up.
4. **Hardware & Physical Computing**: Real-time cognitive mapping at population scale requires massive ML inference — edge AI on social media platforms, FPGA-accelerated behavioral analysis, neuromorphic computing for adaptive pattern detection.
5. **AI-Augmented Intelligence Analysis** (Autonomous Systems): COGINT is the natural extension of AI-augmented intelligence analysis — instead of augmenting human analysts, it augments the cognitive environment itself.
