## What I Think Is Interesting

The convergence of AI trading and institutional adoption is creating a **self-reinforcing cycle**:

1. **Data Advantage:** AI systems generate proprietary data through trading
2. **Model Improvement:** More data → better models → better performance
3. **Market Impact:** Large-scale AI trading influences market dynamics
4. **Regulatory Response:** New regulations emerge to address AI-specific risks

This creates a **moat** for early adopters but also systemic risks if AI models become too correlated.

The most interesting tension: **AI democratization vs. institutional advantage**. Retail traders now have access to AI tools, but institutions have superior data, compute, and regulatory expertise.
