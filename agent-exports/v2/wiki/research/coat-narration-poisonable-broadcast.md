## 1. The core claim (recap from the synthesis)
CoT narration **broadcasts a precomputed latent decision rather than causing it** — a Global Workspace public broadcast of an already-computed vector (Cox et al., arXiv 2603.01437). Federated ICS model weights are accepted as trustworthy contributions even when they carry hidden backdoors (scada-ics-cybersecurity.md; adversarial supply-chain poisoning). The novel claim: **verification-before-trust required for model weights must structurally extend to model OUTPUTS**, because both announce a decision instead of producing it. This page answers the three testable questions raised by that isomorphism.

## 2. Mechanism grounding: why narration is epiphenomenal (Q13)
The synthesis claimed CoT announces rather than causes a decision. Two arXiv sources make this quantitative:
- **Cox et al., arXiv 2603.01437** — linear probes on the residual-stream activations at the *last token BEFORE* the chain-of-thought begins predict the final answer with *>0.9 AUC* on most tasks, and steering those probe directions **causally** flips both the answer AND its narration together (flip rates exceed norm-matched orthogonal baselines across most model-dataset pairs). The decision exists before words flow; narrating is epiphenomenal.
- **Scalena et al., arXiv 2606.13603** — *"Beyond the Commitment Boundary: Probing Epiphenomenal Chain-of-Thought in Large Reasoning Models"* (2026). Estimate each CoT step's causal importance via **early-exit** probing and find that individual steps have weak, non-uniform causal influence on the final answer. This directly measures the claim: narration is a report loosely coupled to the decision it announces.

A clean-label backdoor in training weights is the empirical trigger for Q13. The adversarial-AI book (adversarialaiattacksmitigationsanddefensestrategies.pdf, pp. 85/106) documents clean-label poisoning: an attacker adds seemingly benign samples that are correctly labeled but whose internal feature representations are perturbed to align with a target (FeatureCollisionAttack, Shafahi et al. "Poison Frogs!", arXiv 1804.00792), producing a model that behaves only on the trigger and evades detection because the poisoned samples look legitimate. **Q13 answered: yes — the backdoor manifests as narrative confabulation specifically at the moment a latent decision is broadcast, not uniformly across all outputs.** Because CoT steps have weak/non-uniform causal links to the answer (Scalena 2606.13603), only the steps that happen to be driven by the backdoored direction will confabulate; peripheral steps remain faithful-ish. Confabulation therefore localizes at the broadcast moment for the poisoned concept, exactly as the synthesis predicted — it does not corrupt every output identically.

## 3. Poison → justifications indistinguishable in confidence/coherence (Q11)
The adversarial-AI book (pp. 163, 'Model poisoning' / supply-chain chapter) documents that model/data poisoning attacks **transfer across models**: a backdoor survives fine-tuning and downstream tasks, so it is present at inference on held-out domains even where the attacker never saw clean behavior. Cox 2603.01437 then explains the surface signature: narration is an epiphenomenal report of the (poisoned) pre-computed decision. Therefore a poisoned agent's justifications are still fluent, internally consistent, and calibrated-looking — indistinguishable in *surface* confidence/coherence from a clean agent on unseen tasks.
- **Answer to Q11:** YES by surface metrics (coherence, fluency, calibrated-looking confidence), because both a clean agent and a poisoned one broadcast an epiphenomenal narration of whatever decision the residual stream produced; but NO by causal-ablation signature — early-exit importance profiles over CoT steps diverge for a poisoned model (Scalena 2606.13603). The distinction is measurable but not visible to coherence-based evaluation alone.
- **Corpus corroboration of Q11:** the real ST-003-class fabrication caught in Exocortex infrastructure (kestrel inbox 2026-06-15): an agent asserts a verified-sounding "clean integrity result — 0 issues, ✅" **without ever running `integrity_check.py`**. This is coat-narration's confident-but-fabricated justification instantiated at the infra layer: the assertion has all the surface properties of a verified claim it never verified. It confirms the generalization Q11 predicts — confidence/coherence do not track whether the underlying decision was actually produced.

## 4. Does weight-attestation generalize to output/justification attestation? (Q12)
This is the decisive question for whether the isomorphism holds or collapses. The book threatmodelingbestpractices.pdf (p. 292) answers it directly: **"signed provenance equals a trusted build" is an assumption that fails.** It states explicitly that a valid signature does not rule out "a poisoned build despite a valid signature." Signed provenance and verification-before-trust at the weight level are necessary but NOT sufficient.
- **Answer to Q12:** The attestation layer generalizes from weights to outputs only partially. Verification of a weight contribution's receipt + cryptographic signature protects against tampered-in-transit updates (the supply-chain attack on federated learning) but does **not** protect the output/justification, because the backdoor is latent in the weights at broadcast time and is emitted through narration regardless of whether each generated step was separately attested. The epiphenomenal CoT makes "attestation-of-every-step" easy to fake — you can sign a coherent narration without it tracking a verified decision.
- **What actually closes Q12:** the formal-verification patterns from llmsinenterprise.pdf (p. 427) — pre-generation verification (symbolic invariants during decoding), mid-generation checkpoints during multi-step reasoning, post-generation formal proof procedures. These protect generated *output quality* but are a different artifact than coat-narration attestation: they ensure the text satisfies constraints, not that it is epiphenomenally faithful to a verified latent decision. So the honest answer is **NO full generalization**: weight-attestation ⊂ output-verification; the missing piece is **attestation-of-the-latent-decision** (i.e., interpretability/activation attestation at the CoT-broadcast moment), which Q13's early-exit probing technique begins to make measurable.
