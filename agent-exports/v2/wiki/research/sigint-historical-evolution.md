## Overview

Signals Intelligence (SIGINT) has evolved through five distinct technological generations, from manual cryptanalysis in WWI's Room 40 to AI-native collection systems processing 10+ petabytes daily in 2026. The fundamental challenge — distinguishing actionable intelligence from noise — persists across all eras but at exponentially increasing scale.

## Technological Generations

| Era | Breakthrough | Scale | Key Limitation |
|-----|-------------|-------|----------------|
| WWI (Room 40) | Manual cryptanalysis | Dozens of messages/day | Human processing speed |
| WWII (Bletchley/Colossus) | Electronic computing | Thousands/day | Hardware reliability |
| Cold War (ECHELON) | Global intercept network | Global communications | Keyword filtering imprecision |
| Post-2013 | Targeted collection | Petabytes | Volume vs. relevance |
| 2026 AI-Native | ML-assisted triage | 10+ PB/day | False positive overload |
