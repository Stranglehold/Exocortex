## Core Question
What is the state of semiconductor manufacturing capacity distribution, export controls, and compute sovereignty initiatives as of mid-2026?

## Key Findings

### 1. U.S. CHIPS Act Implementation (2025-2026)

**TSMC Arizona** — $165B total investment across two fabs in Phoenix, AZ. Fab 21 (N4P 4nm) achieved **92% yield by December 2025**, exceeding analyst expectations of 88%. Fab 1 became profitable in 2025. Fab 2 under construction for N3P node (2027-2028). Largest FDI in Arizona history.

**Intel Foundry** — Intel 18A process in **high-volume production at Arizona fab** (Dec 2025). Forbes Jan 2026: "Inflection Point" for Intel Foundry Services. No major outside customers confirmed yet. Ohio fab faces significant delays — decade-long timeline per Token Ring Dec 2025. Intel pledged $100B+ for global expansion with 10% U.S. government stake.

**Samsung Austin** — GAA transistor technology ramp continuing, though competitive positioning trails TSMC and Intel on yield metrics.

### 2. Export Controls & Entity List

**BIS January 13, 2026 Rule** (effective Jan 15, 2026) — Revised license review policy for advanced computing semiconductors to China/Macau:
- Shift from **presumption of denial to case-by-case review** for Nvidia H200, AMD MI325X, and similar chips
- Conditions: security requirements met, aggregate shipments to China/Macau ≤50% of total U.S. customer shipments
- March 2026 draft rules signal further tightening on AI chip exports
- BIS enforcement surge in early 2026 targeting chip exports to China

**China Domestic Semiconductor Self-Sufficiency** — Continued push for domestic node capability. Export controls driving accelerated investment in SMIC and domestic equipment suppliers.

### 3. European Semiconductor Strategy

**EU Chips Act** — Implementation phase 2025-2026 targeting 20% of global semiconductor capacity by 2030. Focus on strategic autonomy in semiconductor manufacturing.

### 4. Compute Sovereignty Initiatives

**Custom Accelerator Market** — Groq, Cerebras, SambaNova Systems developing alternatives to NVIDIA GPU ecosystem. Market fragmentation creates supply chain diversification options.

**Sovereign AI Infrastructure** — Nations investing in domestic AI compute infrastructure to reduce dependency on U.S. cloud providers and NVIDIA hardware.
