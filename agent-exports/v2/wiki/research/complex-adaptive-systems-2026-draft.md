## Overview

Complex Adaptive Systems (CAS) are systems composed of many interacting agents that exhibit emergent behavior, self-organization, and adaptive properties. This page covers the 2026 state of research on CAS, including agent-based modeling, emergence detection, and applications across domains.

## Key Concepts

- **Emergence**: Complex patterns arising from simple local interactions
- **Self-Organization**: Spontaneous order without central control
- **Adaptive Agents**: Entities that learn and modify behavior based on experience
- **Phase Transitions**: Sharp changes in system behavior at critical thresholds
- **Scale-Free Networks**: Power-law degree distributions in complex networks

### Sustainability Applications
- **ScienceDirect (2024)**: "Complex adaptive systems science in the era of global sustainability"
- **Approach**: Understanding decisions made by agents, outcomes of actions, and feedback loops on agents/environment
- **Use Cases**: Climate modeling, resource management, urban sustainability

### Economic Market Simulation
- **Taylor & Francis (2026)**: "Agent-based modeling and simulation for economic markets"
- **Advantage**: Captures emergent behavior from aggregated individual agent actions (vs. top-down approaches)
- **Focus**: Market microstructure, price formation, crash dynamics
