## Key Developments

### 1. AI Trading Algorithms (2026)
- Shift from rule-based to adaptive AI systems
- AI agents that chain multiple analysis steps autonomously
- Multi-step reasoning for complex trading decisions

### 2. High-Frequency Trading (HFT)
- HFT estimated to make up ~55% of US equity trading volume
- ~40% in European equity markets, ~80% in FX futures
- Strategies include arbitrage, order anticipation, momentum ignition
- Concerns about market fragility and volatility (Flash Crash 2010, Aug 2015 crash)
- Aggregate HFT revenues dropped beneath $1B for first time since 2008

### 3. Factor Investing & Smart Beta
- Return as function of uncertainty/risk associated with investment
- Modern Portfolio Theory (MPT) distinguishes idiosyncratic vs systematic risk
- Capital Asset Pricing Model (CAPM) identifies single factor driving returns
- ML used to identify and forecast risk factor behavior

### 4. Institutional Adoption
- Major banks deploying AI for market analysis
- Hedge funds integrating LLMs for sentiment analysis
- Asset managers using AI for portfolio optimization

### 5. Regulatory Landscape
- SEC guidance on AI use in trading
- EU AI Act implications for financial services
- Market manipulation detection using AI
- SEC DERA director: "higher-level reasoning by machines remains an elusive hope"
