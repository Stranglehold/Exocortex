# Data Aggregation & Entity Resolution

**Status: DRAFT**

## Overview

Data aggregation and entity resolution are critical components in the processing of large-scale datasets, especially in contexts like intelligence operations where the resolution of entities across heterogeneous datasets is essential.

## Key Concepts

1. **Data Aggregation**: The process of collecting and combining data from various sources.
2. **Entity Resolution**: The task of identifying and linking records that refer to the same real-world entity across different datasets.

## Research Questions

- How can we efficiently resolve entities in large, heterogeneous datasets?
- What are the best practices for handling missing or conflicting data in aggregation?
- What are the current state-of-the-art techniques for data aggregation and entity resolution?
