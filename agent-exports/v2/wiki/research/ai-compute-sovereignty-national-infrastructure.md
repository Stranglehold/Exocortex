## Core Thesis

AI compute sovereignty has emerged as a central principle of national AI policy by 2026. Nations are building sovereign control over AI training and inference compute infrastructure as a strategic imperative. Between 2018 and 2025, Europe and Central Asia expanded state-backed AI supercomputing clusters from 3 to 44 (Stanford HAI AI Index 2026). The infrastructure underpinning compute sovereignty remains unevenly distributed — US dominates, but China, EU, Japan, India, and Saudi Arabia are actively building parallel capacity.

## Policy Frameworks (Verified)

### United States
- **CHIPS and Science Act $52.7B** — largest semiconductor manufacturing incentive in US history; US share of global semiconductor capacity was 10% in 2022, targeted to triple by 2032 (NIST/semiconductors.org/CRS R47558)
- **BIS Export Controls (Jan 2025–Jan 2026)** — AI Diffusion Rule restricts advanced IC exports to PRC; Jan 2026 revision moved from presumption of denial to case-by-case review with TPP thresholds (Federal Register 2026-00789, Sidley, Cleary Gottlieb)
- **MATCH Act (Apr 2026 draft)** — would extend controls to DUV lithography tools (Global Trade & Sanctions Law Mar 2026)

### European Union
- **EU AI Act (Regulation 2024/1689)** — high-risk AI requirements mandatory August 2, 2026; GPAI model provisions with systemic risk thresholds; €35M/7% global revenue fines (EU Commission, Compliquest 2026)
- **State-backed supercomputing clusters** — 44 clusters across Europe/Central Asia by 2025 (Stanford HAI 2026)

### Other Major Programs
- **Canada AI Sovereign Compute Infrastructure Program (SCIP)** — Budget 2024/2025 investments under Canadian Sovereign AI Compute Strategy (ISED)
- **McKinsey Sovereign AI framework** — sovereignty requires ecosystem integration across energy, compute, data, models, and applications (McKinsey 2026)
- **France, Saudi Arabia, India, Japan** — all building domestic data center capacity and language models to reduce US/China reliance

## Infrastructure Reality (Verified)

### Compute Supply Bottleneck
- **Datacenter power demand:** $1.4T utility capex through 2030 (Business Insider/Fortune Apr 2026)
- **Interconnection queue:** 2,600 GW backlog across ISOs/RTOs with 5-10 year study timelines (enkeiai.com 2026, RMI 2026)
- **AI = ~9% of total US electricity consumption** (Tech Insider 2026)

### Energy-Compute Coupling
- Nuclear/SMR timeline gap — SMRs years from commercial deployment, natural gas bridging near-term
- Grid-edge AI deployment validated at F1=0.98 but remains pilot-phase in production (MDPI 2026)
