## Overview

Autonomous scientific discovery pipelines using AI agents to conduct hypothesis generation, experimental design, data analysis, and peer review without human intervention. Focus on 2025-2026 advances in self-driving laboratories (SDLs), AI co-scientists, and closed-loop discovery systems.

## Key Findings

### The AI Scientist-v2 Milestone
First fully AI-generated peer-review-accepted paper demonstrates end-to-end autonomous scientific discovery is operational. Full pipeline completed: hypothesis to experiment to analysis to manuscript to peer review acceptance.

### SDL 1.0 to SDL 2.0 Transition
Early SDLs proved closed-loop feasibility on narrow tasks. SDL 2.0 addresses: scalability beyond proof-of-concept, cross-domain transferability, reproducibility guarantees, and autonomous orchestration without human coordination.

### Multi-Agent Architecture Dominates
MARS (19 LLM agents, 16 tools) and AI Scientist-v2 both use multi-agent coordination rather than monolithic models. Specialized agents outperform single-agent approaches.

### The Hypothesis Generation Gap
Most SDLs emphasize data-to-idea discovery but struggle with idea-to-data (generating novel testable hypotheses). Closed-loop systems integrating both directions remain rare.
