## Overview

Financial markets analysis encompasses quantitative techniques, alternative data sources, and sector-specific analysis for identifying asymmetric opportunities through data-driven approaches.

## Key Areas

### Quantitative Analysis Techniques

Quantitative market analysis applies statistical and mathematical models to identify mispriced assets, discover trading signals, and manage portfolio risk. Statistical arbitrage — a subset — exploits temporary price divergences between related instruments, relying on mean-reversion, cointegration, and factor models rather than directional bets.

**Factor Models in Asset Pricing:**
- Multi-factor models (Fama-French extensions, Q-factor models)
- Machine learning-enhanced factor generation
- Autonomous factor discovery architectures (FactorMiner, Hubble, Cognitive Alpha Mining)

**Statistical Arbitrage Techniques:**
- Pairs trading and cointegration strategies
- Mean-reversion approaches
- Deep learning approaches for signal generation
- Cross-asset momentum and reversal signals

**Earnings Surprise Modeling:**
- PEAD (Post-Earnings Announcement Drift) anomaly
- NLP-based earnings prediction from transcripts and filings
- Alternative data signals for earnings forecasting

### Options Market Structure

Options market microstructure encompasses implied volatility surface dynamics, gamma exposure (GEX) dealer positioning signals, and flow-based alpha generation. Global options market processes ~108B contracts annually (FIA 2023), with 1.35M distinct US-listed contracts.

**2025-2026 Advances:**
- ML-driven GEX regime detection
- SABR-informed IV surfaces
- Quantitative dealer positioning frameworks
- GAN-based volatility surface reconstruction (IEEE 2025)
- LLM-based gamma exposure inference (71.5% detection rate from raw gamma data)

**Implied Volatility Surface Dynamics:**
- SVI/SSVI parameterization for arbitrage-free surfaces
- Deep hedging approaches
- No-arbitrage surface construction
- Order flow interaction with market maker delta-hedging

### Alternative Data Sources

Alternative data sources provide asymmetric information advantages:
- Satellite imagery (parking lots, oil storage, shipping traffic)
- Web traffic analytics and app usage data
- Patent filing velocity and citation networks
- Job posting analysis for labor market signals
- Credit card transaction data for consumer spending

### Federal Reserve Operations

Federal Reserve operations impact market liquidity and pricing:
- Balance sheet management (QT/QE cycles)
- Repo market mechanics (SOFR-IORB spread as liquidity signal)
- SFR/SRF residual demand modeling
- Reserve scarcity and repo rate volatility

### Sector-Specific Analysis

**Utility Sector:**
- Regulatory dynamics and rate case analysis
- DER (Distributed Energy Resources) integration
- IEC 61850 standards for grid modernization

**Defense Procurement:**
- Multi-year procurement cycles
- Cost-plus vs. fixed-price contract structures
- Technology refresh cycles and obsolescence management

**Semiconductor Capex:**
- EUV lithography capacity constraints
- DUV workaround strategies (SMIC)
- Export control impacts on capital allocation
