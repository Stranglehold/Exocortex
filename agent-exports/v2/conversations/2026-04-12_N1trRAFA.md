What color is the sky?

### 2026-04-12 18:37 UTC · aporia

**The sky is blue** during the day.

This happens because of **Rayleigh scattering**: sunlight contains all colors, but shorter wavelengths (blue and violet) scatter more easily when they hit molecules in Earth's atmosphere. Our eyes are more sensitive to blue than violet, so we perceive the sky as blue.

*Note: The sky can appear different colors depending on conditions:* 🌅
- **Red/orange/pink** at sunrise or sunset (light travels through more atmosphere)
- **Gray/white** when cloudy
- **Black/dark blue** at night
