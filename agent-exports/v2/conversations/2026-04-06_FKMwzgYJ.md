### 2026-04-06 21:31 UTC · aporia

**Approach: Optimized Trial Division**

I used an **O(√n) algorithm** based on these principles:

1. **Edge cases**: Numbers < 2 aren't prime; 2 is the only even prime
2. **Skip evens**: After handling 2, skip all other even numbers (they're divisible by 2)
3. **Square root bound**: Only check divisors up to √n — if n has a factor larger than √n, it must also have one smaller than √n
4. **Odd-only loop**: Check only odd divisors from 3 to √n (step of 2)

**Why this works:**
- For any composite number *n*, at least one prime factor ≤ √n exists
- This reduces complexity from O(n) to O(√n)
