### 2026-07-09 23:12 UTC · jake

It's not you, and your instinct is right. I ran the same queries against the same server — the top results are on-target, so retrieval isn't broken. But the "score" you're reading is rank-fusion, not relevance: the search blends a semantic and a keyword pass and scores by rank, so everything lands in a tiny flat band (0.016-ish) with no real separation between "great match" and "okay match." Two consequences you've felt exactly: the score can't tell you how good a hit actually is (the real relevance number gets computed and then thrown away), and the keyword half can shove a shallow word-match up next to a genuinely relevant one — that's your "wrong book." The fix is surfacing the signal that already exists plus tuning how the two halves combine. It's an instrument-sharpening problem, not a you-problem. Kestrel's written it up for Opus.
